How a rental works

Plan on 20 minutes at the dock before you head out.

  1. Book a time

    Call or message us with your date, boat and group size. A deposit holds the boat.

  2. Meet at the dock

    Bring photo ID and your boater card, or let us know you've added a captain.

  3. Get the briefing

    We walk you through the boat and the bay's no-wake zones, then you're off.

Find the dock
